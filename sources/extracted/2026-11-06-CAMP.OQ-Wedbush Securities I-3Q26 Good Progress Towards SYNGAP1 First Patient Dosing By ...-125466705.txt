     Company Description                                                            
     CAMP4 Therapeutics Corp. is developing regulatory RNA-based therapeutics for metabolic and CNS disorders. Lead candidate CMP-
     CPS-01 in Ph1 is for urea cycle disorders and preclinical-stage candidate CMP-SYNGAP-01 in for SYNGAP1-related disorders.
                                                                                    
     Risks to the Attainment of Our Price Target and Rating:                        
     CAMP4 Therapeutics Corporation is a clinical stage biotechnology company. It faces all the standard risks for this industry, including
     unexpected outcomes from clinical readouts, regulatory uncertainty, competition risks, commercial risks, manufacturing risks, and
     financial risks.
