PRICE TARGET CALCULATION

We value CAMP with DCF methodology assuming a 12% discount rate, reflecting risk that we believe is associated with a clinical-stage
biotechnology company, and a 2% terminal value growth rate and estimated $3.5B terminal FCF value in 2036, driving our 12-18-month
price target of $15.


KEY RISKS TO PRICE TARGET
■  CMP-002 failing to demonstrate clinical signal.
■  Potential safety issues with RAP platform.
■  Clinical trials progressing slower than expected.
■  Potential approval and launch delays.
■  Program(s) reach commercialization, but fall below peak sales expectations due to competition, reimbursement, or pricing pressure.
■  We regard CAMP, as a stock selling for less than $5 per share, as speculative and appropriate for risk-tolerant investors.



 COMPANY DESCRIPTION
CAMP4 Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing regulatory RNA-targeting therapeutics designed to upregulate
gene expression with the goal of restoring healthy protein levels to treat a broad range of genetic diseases, with a focus on CNS
haploinsufficiencies.
