     Valuation.                                                                     
           Camp4 shares trade at a market cap valuation materially discounted to early-stage genetic medicine peers, despite the
     company’s novel RAP platform. Camp4’s foray into the CNS highlights the potential for the company’s ASO-based therapeutic modality
     to target a number of indications, and we expect that further business development ventures will spur expansion of the company’s
     pipeline into other tissues and pathologies as new nucleic acid delivery strategies continue to develop. Data from other players with
     RNA-based gene expression boosters, albeit with differentiated mechanisms of action, should help convince the Street that modest
     iRnicsrkesa.ses in gene expression can provide clinically relevant benefit.    
                                                                                    
         Camp4’s risk profile is similar to other early-stage biotechnology companies harnessing a novel biological mechanism as the
     basis for their platform and asset development. Specific to Camp4, we see regulatory risk, as regulators’ reaction to genome-altering
     therapies has been tepid, and while Camp4’s platform does not target the genome at the level of sequence perturbations, off-target
     effects of regRNA targeting may have variable, unintended effects on gene expression in other parts of the genome if the specificity of
     the RAP platform at the gene enhancer/repressor level is not maintained.
