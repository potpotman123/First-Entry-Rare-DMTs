CMP-002 research package — 9 November 2026

Start with CMP002_research_brief.html. It includes the eight-report CAMP Street baseline, historical research findings, analogue matrix, case studies, candidate/exclusion ledger, model-transferability and investor-claims tables, missing-evidence specification, and linked sources.

research_evidence.json contains the auditable records and data dictionary. It distinguishes observed forecasts, observed actual sales, analyst assumptions, unresolved fields and scientific/data exclusions. It uses null for missing values. The screened candidate set is NOT an exhaustive approval census.

study_protocol.txt records the rules frozen before case-error calculation. Subsequent deviations appear in the report and evidence JSON.

reproduce_calculations.py reproduces the arithmetic using Python 3 without external dependencies. Running it writes calculation_results.json. It does not compute a pooled bias from incompatible records.

Principal conclusion: the recovered evidence does not establish systematic preapproval underforecasting of approval-conditional rare-disease DMT sales. Strict matched 36- and 60-month samples are empty. Public point examples and general launch studies remain contextual. Original historical annual models, documented PoS treatment, matched geography and label scope are the binding missing inputs.

The supplied CAMP reports are a separate, current baseline. Needham's conditional curve is usable as an analyst benchmark, not as an independently validated forecast. Model reconciliation flags and pre-funded warrant capitalization are preserved. No full CAMP valuation, reverse DCF or independent commercial forecast has been performed.

No paid data, bulk scraping, large OCR, outreach or publishing was undertaken. Local source PDFs remain in the user's Downloads folder; the report identifies exact filenames and page references without duplicating the PDFs.
