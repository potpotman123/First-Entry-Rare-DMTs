Frozen before extraction/calculation of case-level forecast errors in this run.
Prior knowledge: the conversation has already identified Spinraza, Brineura and Daybue and broad launch-performance literature. This is prospective rule-setting for this desk study, not blinded preregistration.

Question: signed error in preapproval, approval-conditional net product-sales forecasts for launched first DMTs in rare diseases; no inference about approval probability calibration or investment returns.
Population: US first approvals 2000–2025; 2026 entrants tracked separately; pre-2000 precedents separate. US orphan definition (<200,000 affected or statutory cost-recovery exception, flagged). Orphan oncology excluded from primary cohort.
Universe construction: FDA rare-disease approval communications and orphan/drug approval records; targeted coverage of genetic CNS, neuromuscular, enzyme replacement, inherited metabolic and other rare first-DMT launches. A complete all-orphan census requires a reconciled database export; if not achieved, label ledger screened candidate universe, not exhaustive cohort.
First DMT: first US-approved disease-targeted intervention supported at launch by mechanism and clinical evidence of alteration of disease course/pathophysiology. Record prior transplant, diet and off-label disease-modifying care. Ambiguous DMT classifications separate. First disease-specific symptomatic therapies form adjacent cohort.
Primary forecast vintage: nearest 12 months before approval within 9–15 months; ties select earlier. Secondary: latest dated preapproval forecast. Early-stage: before pivotal results, separate. No postapproval estimates enter preapproval sample.
Primary endpoint: actual/forecast cumulative net sales minus 1 over matched first 36 commercial months. Secondary: 60 months and annual trajectory. Prefer US; matched worldwide analyzed separately. Calendar-year cumulative comparisons are sensitivity analyses, never relabeled 36-month outcomes.
PoS: use disclosed conditional sales or reverse documented scalar probability multiplication only. Unknown risk treatment excludes strict sample. No assumed PoS, no price-target inversion.
Matching: same drug, disease, revenue owner/basis, geography, currency and dates. No later indications. Within-disease expansions flagged. Royalties and partner sales not interchangeable. Peak sales cannot reconstruct an annual curve.
Evidence: original report/model or dated identifiable quotation. Quotes with unresolved PoS/geography/horizon remain contextual. Each original/normalized value retains source, date, locator and status.
Independence: one drug-indication outcome, consensus and individual analyst variation separate. No p-values or pooled claims from incomparable excerpts. Equal weighting primary, dollar weighting secondary if eligible data exist.
Analogue priority fixed before outcome extraction: Spinraza (route/modality), Brineura (CNS delivery), Daybue (neurodevelopmental adoption; adjacent), Kalydeco (diagnosis/genotype/label), Strensiq (metabolic pediatric uptake), Onpattro (RNA and specialist delivery; first-status caveat), Skysona/Zynteglo (delivery and downside; first-status caveats). Other approvals populate screen without selection on sales outcome.
Missingness: absent forecasts are missing, never zero. Strict eligible N can be zero. No inaccessible report treated as read. No extrapolated mature actuals. Missingness and exclusion log required.
Stopping: bounded public discovery and specific followups on promising leads. No paid access, bulk scraping or large OCR. At access boundary finish screening, available case evidence and exact acquisition specification. Report uncompleted census/statistical tests explicitly.
Deliverables: readable report, screened candidate ledger, evidence records, data dictionary, reproducible calculations and limitations. Format may be HTML and machine-readable JSON if no workbook is needed.
